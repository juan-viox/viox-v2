VioX AI Corp ("VioX," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to viox.ai and to the communications services we provide to our clients.

Information we collect

SMS / text messaging consent

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your mobile phone number or your SMS opt-in consent with any third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile opt-in information and consent are never shared with third parties.

Where you have provided your mobile number and agreed to receive text messages, VioX AI Corp may send SMS messages related to your account — including service notifications, appointment and billing reminders, and customer support. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe, or HELP for help. See our Messaging Terms for full details.

We use your mobile number solely to deliver the messages you have consented to receive. We do not use purchased, rented, or third-party contact lists for SMS.

How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and support the services you request.

How we share information

We share information only with service providers who help us operate (for example, messaging and hosting providers), and only as needed to deliver our services, under confidentiality obligations. We may disclose information if required by law. We do not sell your personal information, and we never share mobile opt-in or consent data with third parties for marketing.

Data retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide our services and to meet legal, accounting, or reporting requirements, after which it is deleted or de-identified.

Your choices

  • SMS opt-out: reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts.
  • Access & deletion: contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information.
