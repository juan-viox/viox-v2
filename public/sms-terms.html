SMS / Messaging Terms

Effective date: June 17, 2026

These Messaging Terms describe the SMS text-message program operated by VioX AI Corp ("VioX") for its clients.

Program description

VioX AI Corp sends SMS messages to its business clients who have opted in. Messages include account notifications, appointment and billing reminders, and customer-care / support messages related to the recipient's account and services.

How you opt in (consent)

You opt in by providing your mobile number and giving express written consent — for example, by accepting the SMS consent clause in your VioX AI Corp service agreement at the start of an engagement, which states: "I consent to receive SMS text messages from VioX AI Corp regarding my account, including service notifications, appointment and billing reminders, and customer support. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time, or HELP for help."

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services. We do not use purchased or third-party lists, and we never share your mobile number or opt-in consent with third parties for marketing (see our Privacy Policy).

Message frequency & cost

Message frequency varies based on your account activity. Message and data rates may apply per your mobile carrier plan. VioX AI Corp does not charge for the messages themselves.

Opt-out and help

  • Reply STOP (or STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT) to any message to stop receiving texts. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages.
  • Reply HELP (or INFO) for help, or email support@viox.ai.

Supported carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Service is available on major U.S. carriers.

Privacy

Your information is handled per our Privacy Policy. Mobile opt-in information and consent are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.
